How a stitched order works

Every piece in the shop can be altered, and most can be cut from scratch to your measurements. It usually takes three short visits.

  1. Choose a design, or bring your fabric

    Pick something from the collection, or walk in with fabric you love and a picture of what you want made.

  2. Get measured

    About twenty minutes at the shop. If you can't come in, send us a blouse or kurti that already fits you well.

  3. Try it on

    A trial fitting before the final stitches, and we keep adjusting until it sits right.

  4. Take it home

    Most suits and blouses are ready in about a week. Bridal pieces take three to four weeks, with two fittings.

    From the shop

    We have been dressing women in Behala since 2011. Some come in for a ready kurti on the way home; others sit with us over three visits while a wedding lehenga takes shape.

    Most of what we make starts with a conversation: what the occasion is, how you like to move, where a blouse usually pulls. Then we measure, cut and stitch, and keep fitting until it is right.

    If you see something here you like, ask us about it. If what you have in mind isn't here, send us a picture. That is how many of our favourite pieces began.
