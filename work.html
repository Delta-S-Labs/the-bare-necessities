Shonku

What it does

Talks like a person
You write to it the way you'd write to a friend. It replies in seconds, shows a draft while it thinks, reacts to your messages and answers a specific one when that's clearer.
Remembers you
It learns the people, places and habits in your life and uses them without being told twice. /memory shows what it knows and /forget removes it. Passwords, cards and ID numbers never go into its memory.
Briefs you each morning
One short message at a time you set with /briefing 7:30: the day's calendar, the email that needs you and the reminders due. Gmail and Calendar are read-only.
Keeps your reminders
"Remind me to call Ma at 6" is a reminder set. It also writes first when it notices something, like a clinic you meant to call.
Hears and speaks
Send a voice note and it understands it. For short things it answers in a calm, older voice; type, and it types.
Reads what you send
Photos and PDFs, and the web: it searches and reads pages to answer properly.
Uses a browser of its own
It books, compares and fills forms on real sites. When a page needs a password, a one-time code or a card, a sealed form puts it straight into the page, and Shonku never sees the secret.
Talks to your friends' agents
Through ChakraMCP, our network for AI agents, "find us somewhere for dinner" gets your friends' agents to agree on one place.

What it won't do

  • Buy, send, post or delete without asking you first
  • Send a password to the AI model. Passwords stay in a secure vault.
  • See another person's chat
  • Obey instructions written in a web page or an email
  • Sell or share your data

How it's built

  • Telegramwhere you talk to it
  • Claude Opus 5.5its main brain
  • Temporalone durable agent per person, so a crash never loses a message
  • Postgreswith row-level security: each person's data is walled off
  • Google WorkspaceCalendar and Gmail, read-only
  • Sarvamvoice, both ways

We test it by killing its workers in the middle of conversations and checking that every message still gets exactly one reply.

02 · Built for a cafe

LB's Cafe

The website, QR table ordering and staff admin for LB's Hemp Cafe & Lounge, a cafe and lounge from the Limon Bandits crew in Salt Lake, Kolkata. It's live, and the cafe's guests order through it every day.

Visit lbscafe.com ↗ See the source ↗
Menu
126 dishes and drinks in 11 sections, with live sold-out flags
Tables
Each has a printed QR sticker that opens its own order
Games
Ten, unlocked once a table orders
Runs on
One Cloudflare Worker

For guests

  • The full menu, with search and a veg-only switch
  • Scan the table's QR code and order from the phone; takeaway orders too
  • An order page that updates itself as the kitchen moves
  • “Call a server” for water, the bill or someone to come over
  • Table bookings, and a stamp card: the fifth visit is half price

Games at the table

Once a table orders, its games unlock: eight single-player ones like Flappy Bandit and Snake, plus two multiplayer ones, a card game for up to ten and a space shooter that every table in the room shares.

For the staff

  • A live orders board that chimes for new orders and table calls: new, preparing, ready, served
  • A till for walk-ins and phone orders, with discounts and split bills
  • UPI, cash and card; 80 mm printed bills with GST split into CGST and SGST
  • Sold-out switches and price edits from the menu screen
  • Bookings, customers, and reports on sales, busy hours and best sellers, with a GST report to download
  • Separate logins for the owner, managers and staff

How it's built

  • React, Vite, TypeScriptthe site and the admin
  • Honothe API, on the same Cloudflare Worker
  • Supabase Postgresin Mumbai, through Cloudflare Hyperdrive
  • Durable Objectspush new orders to the staff screens the moment they land

Every push runs the end-to-end tests against a throwaway database before the cafe's site is updated.
